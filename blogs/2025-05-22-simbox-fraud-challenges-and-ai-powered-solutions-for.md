As global communication expands, so does the sophistication of fraud schemes targeting telecom operators. Among these, SIMbox fraud has emerged as one of the most severe challenges facing the industry. This type of fraud costs telecom companies billions of dollars annually, affecting service quality, compromising security, and threatening business growth.
